About Us

Since 2019 · Made by hand

We are three friends and one freezer. We make small batches because that is the only way we know how. Every scoop, every drip, every warm waffle that leaves our kitchen carries the same quiet intention — to make the exact thing you needed without you knowing you needed it. No shortcuts. No noise. Just cold cream, warm chocolate, and the slow afternoon feeling of something made right.
